# What the strong systems do, what we do badly — synthesis (2026-10-08)

Inputs: three subagent surveys (A_streaming_seams.md, B_memory_trust.md, C_camera_eval.md; each marks claims V = read in paper/code, I = inferred — I have not re-read the sources myself) and our own measurements (history_robust, seam_tests, map_consistency, clean_continuation, command_response, feedback_d3/d3b, write_policy). Our system: Wan2.2-TI2V-5B, 4 latents = 16 frames per call, 30 steps, single global timestep, clean decoded->re-encoded history modulated at the target timestep, ReSplat scaffold rebuilt per call (57 ch), ~200 updates x 10 rows per experiment on one GPU.

## 1. Where we stand, failure by failure

## 2. What the field does well that we should copy (ranked by evidence x cost)

Zero GPU
1. Evaluation stack: ViPE/VGGT camera error after Sim(3) with WBench nATE normalisation, per call; R2M-style revisit score with gap-matched non-revisit controls (needs palindromic trajectories; our exact-camera setting makes this easy); VBench subset (subject/background consistency, flicker, smoothness, aesthetic, imaging); HSV-S saturation + contrast; MUSIQ/Q-Align windows with first-vs-last dDrift; relabel the seam ratio as a Chunk-Discontinuity variant and add t-LPIPS. Needed for any submission.
2. Stop the camera-route line for good; state in the paper that the scaffold is the camera channel (AlayaWorld v1.1 / Uni3C precedent) and keep a route only for empty-render frames if at all.

Inference-only on H200 (35 min each)
3. Overlap co-denoising: carry the previous call's last latent as a target that is re-noised to each step's sigma (RePaint-style, scalar timestep), then crossfade the overlapping decoded frames (FramePack soft_append). Rolling Forcing / DF / StreamDiT evidence. Our queued warm-start arm is a weaker cousin (SDEdit init of all 4 targets); keep both.
4. History at small noise at inference (DF stabilisation k~0.02-0.1): add noise to the history latents before each call. Our T1 probe (2026-09) said base Wan prefers co-denoised history; H200 untested.
5. Render-only regime probe: history shrunk to the last latent + anchor, scaffold untouched. Tells whether H200 can follow the scaffold at all when history is weak (mismatch expected; diagnostic, not a solution).

One training (<= 200 updates) — the two candidates with the best evidence
6. Asymmetric-reliability rows: 30-40% rows with history removed entirely (scaffold = sole carrier, AlayaWorld style), the rest with heavier one-step history self-aug; scaffold clean, target clean; same history policy at inference as trained. Attacks "follows history" directly (Lyra 2 and GEAR ablations). Note the tension with the FLM run now training: its 10% scaffold-dropout rows are the CaM/WorldMem pattern the survey ranks weakest for memory use (it is there for CFG calibration, a different goal).
7. Transition training: small history noise sigma_hist ~ U[0,0.2] (WorldPlay) / <= 5% (MAGI-1) on self-generated history rows, loss on target only, random 0-3 overlap latents (LongLive schedule), same at inference. Attacks the seam at the source (targets from pure noise against a t=0 history). Expect seam reduction, not drift elimination.
Lower-evidence third: Robust Dreamer deviation archive on the endpoint frames that feed DA3/ReSplat (one paper, no code, large effect).

Write policy, zero training (test on the fixed-evidence harness in map_consistency first)
8. Align each new endpoint's DA3 depth to the current scaffold render on the valid mask before it enters the source set (GEN3C align_depth); reject endpoints whose photometric/Sampson agreement with the render is poor (ChronoWorld); age-scaled noise on older sources at read. Our M2 did read-time alignment; the write-time alignment + reject is the untested half. This is the one place where a novel, measurable "write gate with an evidence state (validity/confidence channels)" claim could sit.

## 3. Honest positioning for CVPR (deadline 16 Nov)
- System-novelty claims are weak: per-call ReSplat rebuild + scaffold conditioning = GEN3C/Lyra 2 family; latent history + retrieval = WorldPlay/Lyra; CFG with scaffold dropout = standard. Our measured numbers are at a scale the field does not consider final.
- What we have that the field lacks: controlled diagnostics. No paper reports a self-write drift curve vs call index, an arbitration measurement between memory and history (clean-continuation 2x2), a command-response gain for camera routes, a scaffold-seam vs output-seam analysis, or a persistent-map ablation on a trained generator. Echo-Memory is the closest and it is a Minecraft-scale controlled study. An analysis paper ("when does a self-built 3D memory help an autoregressive world model, and why does it not") with the write-gate test (8) and the asymmetric-reliability training (6) as the two interventions is the most defensible shape in five weeks.
